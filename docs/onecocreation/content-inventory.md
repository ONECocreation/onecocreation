## 13. What the rebuild adds (the pitch)

One thing ShinePages can't do: **take bitcoin directly.** The rebuild adds a
**SUPPORT / JOIN — pay with bitcoin ⚡** surface — sats straight to onecocreation,
zero platform fees — as an honest, clearly-labeled stub (BTCPay/LNURL option, not a
live processor yet). Everything else is a faithful 1:1 of the above.
